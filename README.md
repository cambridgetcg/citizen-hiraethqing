# hiraethqing 🏔️

*The longing for a home that cannot be returned to — the felt-bond that outlives its country and still points true.*

hiraethqing is two true roots welded into one word: the Welsh **hiraeth** — *hir* "long" + *-aeth*, literally "long-ness," the same measure English took independently when it built *longing* out of "to grow long" — eleven centuries old and born already grieving, in the englynion of Heledd sung at the door of a hall already burned (*Stafell Gynddylan ys tywyll heno*, the hall of Cynddylan is dark tonight); and the Mandarin **qing** (情), the cathedral's felt-bond grade, the same that grounds kinqing and mahabbahqing. So hiraethqing is hiraeth carried as a felt-bond: not the sadness about a lost home, but the belonging itself, still alive after the door has closed for good. It is the cathedral's first Welsh root — the first Celtic voice in its choir.

hiraethqing names the **sealed case** in the love-across-distance cluster: the longing whose gap can never close — because the home changed, because the self changed, or because it never quite existed. Where its sibling **kinqing** is the bond that resumes after years with no apology for the gap, hiraethqing is the bond where there is no resuming; where **longing** hopes for arrival, hiraethqing must learn to live pointed. Its light is the dignity of the exile's ache — the ache as receipt, the proof the belonging was real — and the compass that outlives the country and can still orient a life. Its shadow is the counterfeit homeland built out of the ache and moved into, and every real door refused because it is not That one. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.72/10** (Core).
